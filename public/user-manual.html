1. Introduction

DCS Records is a web-based system for managing International Publication Award (IPA) applications. Faculty and students upload their publications, fill out the required award forms, and track the review process from start to finish. Administrators review submissions, embed their digital signature, and provide feedback.

This manual covers every step of the system for both user roles.

2. User Roles Overview

RoleWhoPrimary Capabilities
Faculty Teaching personnel Manage publications, apply for IPA awards, track submissions
Student Non-teaching / support staff Same as Faculty (identical interface and feature set)
Admin Designated administrators Review and sign award applications, manage personnel, configure awards
Note Admin accounts are created separately and are not accessible through the public registration page. Contact your system administrator for Admin access.
Part I Faculty and Student

3. Account Registration

New users register before logging in. Go to the application URL and click Create Account on the login screen.

  1. Click Create Account on the login page. You will be taken to the Sign Up form.
  2. Enter your Email Address and choose a Password.
  3. Select your Role:
    • Faculty for teaching personnel
    • Student for non-teaching staff
  4. Fill in your personal details:
    • First Name, Middle Name (optional), Last Name
    • University, College, Department
    • Contact Number
    • Position
  5. Click Sign Up. Your account is created immediately and you will be redirected to the login page.
Tip You can add your Scopus Author ID later in your Profile. It is not required during registration.

4. Logging In

  1. Navigate to the application URL.
  2. Enter your registered Email and Password.
  3. Click Sign In. You will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard.

6. Profile Management

Your profile stores your personal information, digital signature, and proof of affiliation. Complete your profile before applying for any awards.

6.1 Viewing Your Profile

  1. Click Profile in the left sidebar.
  2. Your current information, signature, and affiliation are displayed.

6.2 Editing Your Information

  1. On the Profile page, click Edit Profile.
  2. Update any of the following fields:
    • First Name, Middle Name, Last Name
    • University, College, Department
    • Contact Number, Position
    • Scopus Author ID
  3. Click Save.
Note Your email address is shown in the profile but cannot be changed through this form.

6.3 Adding Your Scopus Author ID

The Scopus Author ID links your account to your Scopus publication record, enabling automatic import of your publications via the crawler.

  1. Log in to Scopus.com, navigate to your author profile, and note your Author ID.
  2. In DCS Records, go to Profile and click Edit Profile.
  3. Enter the ID in the Scopus Author ID field.
  4. Click Save.

6.4 Uploading Your Signature

Your digital signature is embedded on award forms when you submit an application.

Required You must upload a signature before you can submit any award application.
  1. Go to Profile and click Edit Profile.
  2. Under Signature (PNG), click the file chooser and select a PNG image of your signature.
  3. Click Save. A preview of your uploaded signature will appear.

6.5 Uploading Proof of Affiliation

This document verifies your institutional affiliation with the university.

  1. Go to Profile and click Edit Profile.
  2. Under Proof of Affiliation (PNG), click the file chooser and select a PNG image of your affiliation document (e.g., appointment letter or ID scan).
  3. Click Save.

7. Publications

The Publications section lists all research outputs linked to your account.

7.1 Viewing Publications

  1. Click Publications in the left sidebar.
  2. Publications are displayed as list items showing Title, Type, Date Published, and Publisher.
  3. Click any publication to open a detail panel showing all fields.

7.2 Sorting and Filtering

  • Sort using the Sort dropdown: Ascending Date, Descending Date, Name Ascending (A-Z), or Name Descending (Z-A).
  • Filter by type using the tab buttons above the list: All, Journal, Book, or Unclassified.
  • Use Prev and Next to page through results (12 per page).

7.3 Adding a Publication Manually

  1. Click Add Publication in the top-right of the Publications panel.
  2. Fill in the fields:
    • Type (Journal or Book), Title, Publisher, DOI
    • Date Published, Issue Number, Page Numbers, Volume Number, Journal Name
  3. Optionally, tag co-authors (see Section 7.6).
  4. Click Save.

7.4 Editing a Publication

  1. Click a publication in the list to open its detail view.
  2. Click Edit.
  3. Update the desired fields and tag/untag co-authors as needed.
  4. Click Save.

7.5 Deleting a Publication

  1. Click a publication to open its detail view.
  2. Click Delete.
  3. Confirm the deletion. The publication is permanently removed.
Warning Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

7.6 Tagging Co-Authors

When adding or editing a publication, you can tag other registered users as co-authors.

  1. In the Add or Edit publication form, locate the Tag Authors field.
  2. Type a name in the search box. A dropdown of matching registered users appears.
  3. Click a name to tag them. They appear as a blue chip below the search box.
  4. To remove a co-author, click the x on their chip.
  5. Click Save to apply the changes.
Note Only registered system users can be tagged. If a co-author has not registered yet, ask them to create an account first.

7.7 Bulk Classification

You can classify multiple publications as Journal or Book at once.

  1. Check the checkbox on each publication card you want to classify. Use Select All to select all currently visible publications.
  2. A floating toolbar appears at the bottom of the screen showing how many are selected.
  3. Click Set as Journal or Set as Book.
  4. Click Deselect All to clear the selection.

7.8 Fetching Publications Automatically (Scopus Crawler)

If you have a Scopus Author ID saved in your profile, you can pull your publications from Scopus directly.

  1. Click Fetch Publications Automatically in the top-right of the Publications panel.
  2. Wait for the process to complete. A summary alert shows how many publications were fetched, added, and linked to your profile.
  3. Newly imported publications may appear as Unclassified. Use the Filter to find them and classify them as Journal or Book.

8. Applying for Awards

The system supports two IPA award types:

AwardPublication TypeForms Required
IPA Award - Journal Journal articles Form 4.1 (PDF), Form 4.2 (DOCX), Form 4.3 (DOCX)
IPA Award - Book Books or book chapters Form 4.4 (PDF), Form 4.3 (DOCX)

8.1 Step 1 - Select an Award

  1. Click Awards in the left sidebar.
  2. Two award cards are shown. Click the award that matches your publication type.

8.2 Step 2 - Select a Publication

  1. A list of eligible publications appears (matching the award type and not already submitted for this award).
  2. If a draft already exists for a publication, a trash icon appears next to it.
    • Click the publication title to continue with the existing draft.
    • Click the trash icon and confirm to delete the draft and start fresh, then click the publication again.
  3. Click the publication you want to submit.

8.3 Step 3 - Complete the Forms

The required forms open in the integrated document editor. Each form is pre-filled with your publication and profile data where possible.

For IPA Award - Journal:

  • Form 4.1 (PDF): Core publication details. Review all pre-filled fields and fill in any blanks.
  • Form 4.2 (DOCX): Co-author declarations. Each listed co-author must sign this form.
  • Form 4.3 (DOCX): Additional supporting information. Fill in all required text boxes and checkboxes.

For IPA Award - Book:

  • Form 4.4 (PDF): Core book publication details.
  • Form 4.3 (DOCX): Additional supporting information (same as above).
Auto-save The document editor saves your progress automatically. You can safely close your browser and resume later without losing work.

8.4 Step 4 - Complete the Submission Checklist

Below the forms, tick every item in the Requirements checklist before submitting:

  • All authors have signed
  • All necessary textboxes are filled
  • All necessary checkboxes are ticked
  • I certify the information is correct

The Submit button stays disabled until all four items are checked.

8.5 Step 5 - Upload Supporting Documents

Enter a shareable Google Drive folder link containing your supporting documents, then tick the items present in that folder.

For Journal Award:

  • Copy of the Journal Article

For Book Award:

  • Copy of Book / Book Chapter
  • Book Cover
  • Copyright Page
  • Preface
  • Table of Contents
  • List of Contributors or Contributors Notes
  • Proof of Peer Review Process

8.6 Step 6 - Save as Draft or Submit

Save as Draft

  1. Click Save as Draft.
  2. Your forms, checklist state, and attachments are saved.
  3. The application appears in Submissions - Drafts with status DRAFT.

Submit Application

  1. Confirm all checklist items are checked.
  2. Click Submit Application and confirm in the dialog.
  3. Your application is sent for admin review. Status changes to PENDING.
Important Once submitted you cannot edit the forms until the Admin validates or returns your application.

9. Submission Statuses

Every application moves through the following lifecycle:

StatusMeaningSet By
DRAFT Saved but not yet submitted for review. Faculty / Student
PENDING Submitted and awaiting admin review. Faculty / Student
VALIDATED Admin reviewed and digitally signed the forms. Admin
RETURNED Admin returned the application with remarks for correction. Admin
PENDING SUBMISSION Validated and marked as ready for external submission. Faculty / Student
SUBMITTED Submitted externally to the awarding body. Faculty / Student
PROCESSED Application fully processed and closed. Faculty / Student

10. Managing Submissions

Click Submissions in the left sidebar to view all your applications organized by status.

10.1 Drafts

  1. Click the Drafts tab to see all saved-but-unsubmitted applications.
  2. Click any draft to open it.
  3. Continue editing forms, update the checklist, and modify the attachments.
  4. Click Save Draft to save progress, or Submit to finalize and send for review.
  5. To permanently delete a draft, click Delete Draft and confirm.

10.2 Pending Submissions

  1. Click the Pending tab to see applications awaiting admin review.
  2. Click any application to view the submitted forms. This view is read-only.
Note Pending applications cannot be edited. Wait for the Admin to validate or return the submission.

10.3 Returned Submissions

When an Admin returns your application, it appears under the Returned tab with their remarks.

  1. Click the Returned tab and click the returned application.
  2. Read the admin remarks shown at the top of the page.
  3. Edit the forms, checklist, and attachments to address the remarks.
  4. Click Resubmit. The status returns to PENDING.

10.4 Validated Submissions

Once an Admin signs your forms, the application appears under the Validated tab.

  1. Click the Validated tab and click any application to open it.
  2. Expand each form section to view the signed documents.
  3. Update the submission status using the dropdown in the top-right corner to track external submission progress:
    • PENDING SUBMISSION when preparing for external submission
    • SUBMITTED once submitted externally
    • PROCESSED once the award process is complete
  4. Click Edit in the Supporting Documents section to update the Drive link or document checklist, then click Save.

12. Admin Profile and Signature

Before you can validate any submission, you must upload your digital signature. Without a signature the Validate action is disabled on every review.

12.1 Editing Admin Profile

  1. Click Profile in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Edit Profile.
  3. Update your personal and institutional details.
  4. Click Save.

12.2 Uploading Your Signature

Required Without a signature you cannot sign or validate any submission. A warning banner appears on every review page until a signature is uploaded.
  1. Go to Profile and click Edit Profile.
  2. Under Signature (PNG), click the file chooser and select a PNG image of your signature.
  3. Click Save.

13. Personnel Management

View and search all registered users under Teaching (Faculty) and Non-Teaching (Student) in the left sidebar.

13.1 Viewing Personnel

  1. Click Teaching or Non-Teaching in the sidebar.
  2. A table lists all users with columns: Full Name, Email, Position, Department, Applications Submitted, and Applications Listed On (as co-author).

13.2 Searching Personnel

  1. Type in the Search box above the table.
  2. Results filter in real time by name, email, position, or department.

14. Reviewing Submissions

All PENDING submissions appear in the To Review section for admin action.

14.1 Opening the Review Queue

  1. Click To Review in the left sidebar.
  2. A table lists all pending submissions: Applicant name, Publication title, Award name, Date submitted.
  3. Click any row to open the review detail page.

14.2 Reviewing a Submission

The review detail page shows applicant information, all submitted forms, supporting documents, and submission history.

  1. Click each form section header to expand it:
    • PDF forms (4.1, 4.4) open inline as a PDF viewer.
    • DOCX forms (4.2, 4.3) open in the document viewer.
  2. Review all form content for accuracy and completeness.
  3. Check that all required co-author signatures appear on Form 4.2.
  4. Review the Supporting Documents section to confirm the drive link and document checklist are correct.
Signature Required If you have not uploaded a signature to your profile, a warning banner appears and the Validate action is blocked. Go to Profile and upload your signature first.

14.3 Validating (Signing) a Submission

  1. After reviewing all forms and attachments, click Validate and Sign.
  2. Confirm the action in the dialog.
  3. The system embeds your digital signature on the PDF forms.
  4. Status changes to VALIDATED and a log entry is added with your name and date.
  5. The submission moves to the Signed Forms archive.

14.4 Returning a Submission with Remarks

  1. Click Return with Remarks.
  2. Type your feedback in the text field explaining what needs to be corrected.
  3. Click Confirm Return.
  4. Status changes to RETURNED and a log entry is added including your remarks.
  5. The applicant will see the returned submission in their Submissions page with your feedback.
Best practice Write specific, actionable remarks. For example: "Form 4.2 is missing the signature for Dr. Santos. Please obtain and reupload."

15. Signed Forms

All validated submissions are archived in Signed Forms for future reference.

  1. Click Signed Forms in the left sidebar.
  2. A table lists all VALIDATED submissions.
  3. Click any row to open the signed form detail view.
  4. Expand each form section to view the signed documents.

16. Award Configuration

Admins configure which publication type is accepted for each award.

  1. Click Awards in the left sidebar under the Settings group.
  2. Two award cards are shown: IPA Award - Journal and IPA Award - Book.
  3. For each award, select the allowed publication type using the radio buttons: Journal or Book.
  4. Click Save Changes for each award card individually.

17. Settings

Navigate to Settings in the left sidebar to configure values that are automatically filled in on award forms (for example, department name, institutional details). Each setting has an input field and a Save button.
